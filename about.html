About Raj Agencies

Company Profile

Trusted automotive spare parts distribution since 1997

Raj Agencies was established in 1997 by Mr. Alpesh D. Jogani and Mr. Rakesh V. Jogani. The company operates across Gujarat and works with a network of more than 1,200 key retailers.

The portfolio covers Light Commercial Vehicles, Heavy Commercial Vehicles, passenger cars, tractors, three-wheelers, industrial hardware and related components. The company focuses on quality, dependable service, professional operations and long-term relationships.

Vision

To grow as a leading trading organization in the Indian automobile sector while increasing profitability and improving the lives of stakeholders.

Mission

  • Deliver reliable spare parts and service with consistency.
  • Build long-lasting relationships with retailers, distributors and customers.
  • Improve operational excellence, innovation and market responsiveness.
  • Support the growth and prosperity of customers, employees and partners.
